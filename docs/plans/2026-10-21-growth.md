# Growth round: hooks, safe zones, formats, sound, board v2, new starts

Chosen by the owner on 2026-10-21 (two rounds of questions). Nothing paid: every part runs on the Mac
(ffmpeg, macOS Vision, whisper.cpp, Claude through the owner's own login).

## Phase 1 — the reel itself

- **Hook check, on every master, automatically.** `npm run hook -- check reel.json` samples the first 3 s
  (Instagram reports a 3-second skip rate), reads the text on each frame (Vision), measures motion, and checks the
  hook's words against the reading floor. It writes `hook.json` and `hook/strip.jpg`. The render runs it after the
  master; Claude looks at the strip, saves a verdict (score 1–10, a line, a rewrite if it is weak) with
  `npm run hook -- verdict`, and proposes the fix with AskUserQuestion, waiting for a yes. The studio shows the card on
  the master and offers "Fix the hook" (sends the rewrite to Claude) and "Make 3 hooks".
- **3 hooks.** Only beat 1 changes. Claude composes three openings with different angles (a number, a question, a
  bold claim, a curiosity gap) at beat 1's exact length, renders them as clips, and `npm run hook -- variants`
  joins a full master for each from the existing clips (their audio slices included) and measures each one. The
  studio plays them side by side: **Use this one** swaps it in without a re-render, and **A/B** marks two to post;
  the Performance page then records which variant each reading belongs to and says which held more viewers at 3 s.
- **Instagram safe zones.** An overlay on the master (9:16): the Reels header, the button column, the caption and
  audio line, and the profile grid's 3:4 crop. The hook check also reads the text on the whole master (every 0.5 s)
  and lists any line that sits under Instagram's UI, with its time.
  Zones, as fractions of the frame: top 12%, bottom 20%, right 16% below 55% of the height (from Meta's published
  percentages and common guides; no single official pixel spec).
- **Every format.** "Make the other formats" asks Claude to lay the reel out again for 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1 in
  `formats/<w>x<h>/`, render each, and the master view switches between them.

## Phase 2 — sound

- **Cuts on the music.** The render already snaps cuts to the music's beat (`"music": {"sync": "snap"}`); the studio
  gets a switch for it and shows how many cuts landed on a beat.
- **Auto sound cues** from the owner's own library, on by default when the library has approved sounds.

## Phase 3 — the client board

- **Pin + draw on a frame:** the client pauses, clicks a spot or draws a circle or arrow; Claude gets the second,
  the spot and the picture.
- **Before / after:** a changed item shows the version the client saw next to the new one, with their note.
- **The owner's brand** on the board: logo, colours and name, set once in the studio.

## Phase 4 — new ways to start

- **Long video → reels:** local whisper, Claude finds the 3–5 strongest moments, each becomes a reel with the real
  audio as its voiceover.
- **Brand from a website:** colours, fonts and logo read from the page; a drafted personality.
- **Blog / post → reel:** a URL becomes a script and a beat plan.
