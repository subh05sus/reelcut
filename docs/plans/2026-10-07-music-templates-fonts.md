# Music bed, templates from liked beats, more fonts: decisions (2026-10-07)

The owner chose items 3 and 6 from the feature list, plus "add much more fonts". An item "12" was a typo.

## Music bed (3)
- **Source:** your library first. If nothing fits, Claude searches free-licence sites (Pixabay Music, Free Music Archive CC0/CC-BY, Incompetech) and downloads a track into the library with its URL, licence and credit line. If that fails too, it generates one with Higgsfield, labelled AI-generated.
- **Sync, chosen per reel in Step 0:**
  - Music fits the cut: beat lengths stay exact, and the track's offset is chosen so its strongest beats land on cuts.
  - Cuts snap to beats: each cut moves to the nearest beat, by at most 0.15 s.

## Templates from what you liked (6)
- A beat rated "Works" is saved automatically as a personal pattern. Its words, numbers and assets are marked as slots; the layout and motion stay.
- Step 4 offers personal patterns next to the built-in ones, best-rated first. The studio has a Patterns view to rename, tag or remove them.

## Fonts
- A bundled library of fonts with German umlauts and ß: grotesk sans, display and condensed, editorial serifs, handwritten, mono and rounded.
- Stored inside the skill, so it works offline and renders identically every time. A clip loads only the fonts it uses.
- About 12 curated pairings (headline + body + accent + mono). Step 0 offers three. A beat can override a single font. Learnings record which pairings are kept.
