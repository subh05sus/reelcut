# Clawd, the Claude mascot, in reels: decisions

Answers from the owner, dated. Each question is asked once.

## Round 1 (2026-10-13)

- **When:** automatically in reels about Claude (Claude, Claude Code, its connectors, skills, MCP, scheduled tasks).
  Step 0 confirms it in one line. It is a Mascot setting in the personality, so another personality can switch it
  off or put Clawd in every reel.
- **Role:** host, acting out the steps. Clawd opens the hook, does each step itself (plugs in the connector, hands
  Claude the skill, taps Post), reacts when a check lands and waves at the call to action. It sits out beats where
  the UI needs the whole frame.
- **Motion:** like Anthropic's own Clawd clips: a quick crouch before a hop, a real gravity arc, legs that step,
  poses that hold. Only Clawd moves this way; type, cards and UI stay on Apple's springs. On a stepped style
  (Claymation, Paper Cutout…) it plays on that style's held frames like everything else.
- **Abilities, all four in the first version:** acting on the UI (tap, plug a connector cable, drag a file into the
  chat, hold a phone), reacting to the voice (on a spoken word, looking at each line as it lands, bobbing while the
  voice talks), big moments (confetti stomp, flag wave, a workout power-up, a sign with one word, sleep then wake, a
  sweat drop), and costumes and props (hats, clawd-react's ten accessories, held props).
- **Any personality, any design** (owner's note): Clawd works in all twenty styles and every look, taking each
  style's material (clay, paper, outline, ink, voxel, glitch…) while its silhouette and its orange never change.

## Round 2 (2026-10-13)

- **Design:** Anthropic's minimal Clawd, as in its own clips and Claude Code's banner: an 8×6 body, two square
  eyes, arms at mid-height, four legs, no mouth. Expressions, props and effects are drawn on a half-unit grid (the
  way Anthropic's flag clip draws its flag at half the body's pixel).
- **The mascot pack** (`claude-mascot-pack-SVG`, 75 pixel poses, provided by the owner): ships with reelcut. Its
  poses are re-cast with the minimal Clawd: each keeps its props and effects (lightbulb, laptop, Zs, confetti,
  tears…) at the pack's proportions, and Clawd takes the pose's arms, eyes, gaze and legs. The pack's own Clawd
  (bigger, with a mouth) never appears in a reel. Source: handed over by the owner, who cleared it for the repo.
- **Sounds:** Clawd's own 8-bit blips (step, hop, land, tap, pop, cheer, confetti), synthesised for reelcut, so they
  carry no licence. Mixed under the voice like every effect.
- **Extras, all four:** mini Clawds for subagents (parallel work runs off and comes back), overlay clips (every move
  rendered as a transparent clip for the owner's own editor), a Clawd page in the studio (every pose and move
  playing in any style; starred ones are preferred), and a hatching egg (a new tool or skill hatches).

## Rules this sets

- Clawd is the one character reelcut draws although it is Anthropic's: the kit draws it from one fixed spec (the
  shape, the proportions, `#d97757`). A beat poses, animates, dresses and lights Clawd; it never redraws, recolours or
  reshapes it. Everything else about identity assets stands: no logo is ever drawn.
- Clawd is deterministic like everything else: seeded blinks, no clocks, no `repeat: -1`.
