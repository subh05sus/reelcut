# Personality: decisions

A "Personality" page in the studio: a design-crafter wizard that gives every generated reel a consistent personality.
Answers from the owner, dated. Each question is asked once.

## Round 1 (2026-10-07)

- **Styles:** choose up to 3 of 20: Paper Cutout, Claymation, Stop Motion, Hand-Drawn, Flat Vector, Linear/Hairline,
  Kinetic Typography, Editorial, Collage, Brutalist, Swiss, Minimal, 3D, Isometric, Liquid, Ink/Paint, UI/Product,
  Retro/VHS, Glitch, Cinematic.
- **Colour schemes per style**, with an alert when a scheme does not suit a style. **Font selections per style.**
- **Style depth: both, staged.** Ship the wizard and the personality as direction now (it steers looks, palette, fonts,
  motion and moves on the existing kit); add real render styles (kit looks with their own textures, shapes, motion
  grammar) in waves. A style without its render style yet is marked as such.
- **Scope:** several saved personalities (per brand or series), one of them the default. Step 0 asks which one, or
  skips the question when there is only one.
- **Previews:** not live in-browser beats. The 20 style previews are rendered (HyperFrames) once and saved; the wizard
  shows the saved renders. An option renders mini beats on demand for the owner's own combination.
- **Extra steps:** Motion personality · Texture & finish · Signature moves · Brand import.

## Round 2 (2026-10-07)

- **More steps:** Copy & voice tone · Sound personality · Pacing & density · Guardrails.
- **Helpers:** Style mixer (weights, and which beat types get which style) · Learn from my taste (References +
  "Works" ratings) · Surprise me + lock. (Mood board: not chosen.)
- **Preview set:** both — the same sample in all 20 (comparable) and each style's ideal demo (what it is best at).
- **Preview format:** 1:1, a 4 s loop MP4 plus a poster frame.

## Round 3 (2026-10-08)

- **Layout:** a steps rail on the left (checkmarks), the step's choices in the middle, a live personality summary on
  the right (styles, palette, fonts, warnings). Phone: a top progress bar, the summary in a bottom sheet.
- **Conflicts:** warn with the reason and a one-click fix that keeps the owner's hue; they may keep it anyway.
  Contrast failures (unreadable text) are red and block saving until fixed.
- **Palettes:** 6–8 curated, contrast-checked schemes per style, plus the brand's colours (from Brand import) adapted to
  each style, plus custom ground / ink / accent pickers.
- **Applying:** the default personality is applied to every reel; Step 0 confirms it in one line and skips every
  question it answers. An answer given in Step 0 still wins for that reel.
